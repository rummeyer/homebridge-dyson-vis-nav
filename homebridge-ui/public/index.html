Authorise access to your MyDyson account. There is nothing to do in the MyDyson app itself — this page asks Dyson to email you a one-time code, and exchanges it for a token stored alongside this plugin’s data. Your password is not saved by this step.

Step 1 — Enter your MyDyson credentials

Fill in Email Address and Password in the configuration form below this box, then come back up here.

Step 2 — Request a code

Dyson sends an email titled “Log in to your MyDyson App”.

Step 3 — Enter the code from the email
